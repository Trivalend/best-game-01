Emberdrift is a top-down arena survival shooter: you are a lone ember-wisp, and your light is both your sight and your weapon. Fight waves of drifters, spitters and brutes in the dark, collect the embers they drop to grow your light, pick an upgrade between waves, and slay the Emberlord on wave 10. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make the best game" bounty, $1,000 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
cd game
python3 -m http.server 8080
# open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome
```
